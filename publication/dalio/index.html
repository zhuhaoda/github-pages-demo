DALIO: Degeneracy-Aware, Efficient LiDAR-Inertial Odometry With Adaptive Multiresolution Mapping

Haoda Zhu*, Fangcheng Zhu*, Yuhao Fang, Jiale Han, Yunfan Ren, Siqi Liang, Longji Yin, Jie Mei, Fu Zhang
* Equal contribution

IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics · 2026

T-MECH 2026First author
PaperPDFDesign notesProject page

Overview

Degeneracy-aware LiDAR–inertial odometry with an adaptive multi-resolution map for efficient state estimation.

DALIO combines degeneracy-aware batch selection and suppression with an adaptive multi-resolution mapping strategy, targeting efficient and robust localization on resource-constrained robotic platforms.
